_Welcome!_ I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and
Engineering at the [Pennsylvania State University](https://www.eecs.psu.edu). I
am advised by [Prof.  Patrick McDaniel](http://patrickmcdaniel.org) and am lead
graduate student of the Systems and Internet Infrastructure Security Laboratory
(SIIS).

My research focuses on the security of machine learning systems and using
machine learning for security. I am exploring how machine learning systems can
be abused by adversaries and techniques to better secure them. I am also
investigating how to leverage machine learning to solve challenging security
problems.
